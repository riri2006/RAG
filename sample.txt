It was a rainy evening when Aarav noticed a small, old library at the end of his street. He had lived there for years, but he had never seen it before. Curious, he pushed open the wooden door.

Inside, the library was quiet and warm. Thousands of books filled the tall shelves, and a strange golden clock hung on the wall. An old librarian smiled at him and said, “You may choose one book, but remember—every story has a lesson.”

Aarav picked a dusty blue book titled *The Door That Wasn't There*. As soon as he opened it, the lights flickered. Suddenly, he heard a soft knocking sound coming from behind one of the bookshelves.

He moved closer and discovered a small hidden door.

With a little hesitation, Aarav opened it. Behind the door was a beautiful garden filled with glowing flowers and hundreds of butterflies. In the center stood a tree with a note hanging from its branches.

It read, **“The greatest discoveries begin with curiosity.”**

Aarav smiled and returned to the library. When he looked back, the hidden door had disappeared.

The next morning, he went back to the same street—but the library was nowhere to be found.

From that day on, Aarav never ignored his curiosity. He understood that sometimes the most ordinary moments can lead to extraordinary adventures.
